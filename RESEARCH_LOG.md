# Research Log

## Project

Predicting Non-Use of Skilled Birth Attendance in Nigeria Using Pre-Delivery Information: Evidence from the 2024 Nigeria Demographic and Health Survey

## Purpose

This log documents the progress of the research project from initial setup through analysis, modelling, manuscript preparation, and publication.

---

## Week 1 — Research Proposal Development

### Completed
- Obtained approval for the research topic.
- Obtained DHS Program authorisation to access the 2023–24 Nigeria Demographic and Health Survey datasets.
- Downloaded the relevant NDHS datasets for future analysis.
- Created the GitHub repository and organised the project folders.
- Documented DHS data protection and repository management rules.
- Developed the research work plan.
- Conducted a preliminary literature review on skilled birth attendance, maternal healthcare access and machine-learning prediction.
- Identified and documented the proposed research gap based on existing literature.
- Developed the research problem, research questions, hypotheses and objectives.
- Drafted the proposed methodology, including data preparation, candidate models, validation, evaluation and interpretability.
- Identified potential data leakage associated with using information available only during or after childbirth.
- Completed the first full draft of the research proposal.
- Submitted the research proposal through Google Colab.

### In Progress
- Final verification of supporting literature documentation and repository requirements.

### Challenges
- Identifying a meaningful research gap while ensuring that the proposed study does not duplicate existing research.
- Distinguishing statistical association from machine-learning prediction.
- Determining which NDHS variables would realistically be available before childbirth.
- Developing a methodology that addresses data leakage, model validation, calibration and subgroup performance.
- Ensuring that the proposal does not present planned analyses as completed findings.

### Decisions Made
- Retain the mentor-approved research topic.
- Focus on predicting non-use of skilled birth attendance in Nigeria.
- Use the 2023–24 NDHS as the proposed primary data source.
- Restrict predictive features to information that would be available before childbirth.
- Exclude actual place of delivery and other variables that could introduce outcome leakage.
- Use interpretable logistic regression as a baseline and compare it with selected machine-learning models.
- Evaluate discrimination, class-specific performance and calibration.
- Examine geographic and socioeconomic differences in model performance.
- Keep restricted DHS respondent-level data out of the public GitHub repository.
- Use Python and Jupyter notebooks for the research analysis.
