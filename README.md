# Predicting Non-Use of Skilled Birth Attendance in Nigeria

## Project Overview

This research project investigates the non-use of skilled birth attendance in Nigeria using information available before delivery.

The study will use data from the 2024 Nigeria Demographic and Health Survey (NDHS) to examine demographic, socioeconomic, and healthcare-access factors associated with skilled birth attendance and explore the use of machine learning to identify women who may be less likely to receive skilled attendance during delivery.

## Research Topic

**Predicting Non-Use of Skilled Birth Attendance in Nigeria Using Pre-Delivery Information: Evidence from the 2024 Nigeria Demographic and Health Survey**

## Data Source

Nigeria Demographic and Health Survey (NDHS) 2024 — The DHS Program.

The DHS datasets used for this research are subject to the DHS Program's terms of use and will not be uploaded or redistributed through this repository.
