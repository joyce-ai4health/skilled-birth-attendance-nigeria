# Literature Review

This folder contains the literature reviewed for the research project.

## Purpose

The literature review will support the identification and confirmation of the research gap and provide evidence for the research proposal and final paper.

The review will focus on studies related to:

- Skilled birth attendance in Nigeria
- Maternal healthcare utilisation in Nigeria
- Skilled birth attendance in Sub-Saharan Africa
- Machine learning for predicting skilled birth attendance
- Explainable machine learning in maternal health
- Demographic and socioeconomic determinants of skilled birth attendance
- Geographic and socioeconomic inequalities in maternal healthcare
- Machine-learning validation, calibration, and subgroup performance

## Research Guide Requirement

The research guide requires a systematic review of approximately 8–12 key studies, including both relevant foundational and recent research.

For each selected study, the following information will be documented:

- Full citation
- Publication year
- Country or study setting
- Dataset and sample size
- Research objective
- Methods and models used
- Evaluation metrics
- Main findings
- Strengths
- Limitations
- Research gap identified
- Relevance to the proposed study
