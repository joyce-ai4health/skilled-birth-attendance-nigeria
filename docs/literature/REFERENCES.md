# Research Literature Tracker

## Research Topic

Predicting Non-Use of Skilled Birth Attendance in Nigeria Using Pre-Delivery Information: Evidence from the 2024 Nigeria Demographic and Health Survey

## Purpose

This document tracks the research papers and supporting materials reviewed for the literature review.

The final literature review will include approximately 8–12 key studies as required by the Research Guide.

## Literature Review Categories

Studies will be organised under the following themes:

1. Skilled birth attendance and maternal healthcare in Nigeria
2. Determinants of skilled birth attendance
3. Machine learning for skilled birth attendance
4. Explainable machine learning in maternal and public health
5. Geographic and socioeconomic inequalities in maternal healthcare
6. Machine-learning validation, calibration, and generalisation
